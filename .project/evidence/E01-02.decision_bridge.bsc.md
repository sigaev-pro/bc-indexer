# E01-02.decision_bridge: BSC block (part of #17)

This block wires the shared decision core (block 0, PR #157) into the BSC
import path. It adds:
- the native decision step and marker patch (patch 07);
- the child-root barrier;
- the import-path calls `Intend`, `Resolve`, `RejectIntent` and `Cleanup`;
- the switch of the BSC admission to `admission.OpenWithCleanup` with a
  `Purge` adapter;
- the recovery driver, which ends in `decision.Bridge.GoLive`;
- the BSC fork choice and finality result as decision evidence;
- the proof that a block already in the chain opens no new observation after
  a restart.

The design is in the
[BSC decision README](../../adapters/bsc/decision/native/README.md). The qa
AC-CAP-07 matrix for the private BSC slot is in "AC matrix (BSC)" below.

Scope: private local evidence on the accepted private BSC Parlia fixture
(chain 714, test-only), plus unit tests, portable controls and mutants. Every
public cell (`bsc-mainnet`, `bsc-testnet`) stays NOT_RUN for E06 and E07
(D96, D98, D99). #17 stays open until every client block is accepted.

## What the records show

Values are from `run-02` (first 8 hex digits).

- **Live import.** Each block gets one IMPORT decision, in this order:
  EXEC_READY, barrier, evidence, intent, block batch, head batch, decided.

  | Block | EXEC_READY | Root | Evidence | Intent | NATIVE_DECIDED |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | `6c150bcd` | `b27a0f67` | `37167f85` | `b7ecb188` | `ab9ce628` |
  | 2 | `00c3ba63` | `b27a0f67` | `b999e67e` | `0ae1c83a` | `556fdf70` |

  Both blocks lie before Plato (block 7), so the justified and the finalized
  block are the genesis. The probe mirror applied decision 0; `Cleanup` then
  removed its marker and pruned its records, so one obligation remains.
- **Closing step.** Every admission DECIDED record binds the member's
  EXEC_READY digest and the decision digest. Afterwards no observation is
  open, and no capsule, log or pin remains (`OpenWithCleanup`).
- **Crash points.** Each crash point recovers as the README table says. The
  driver recomputes every intent, NATIVE_DECIDED, rejected-intent, marker,
  admission DECIDED, evidence and bad-block digest independently.
  - Block 1 has exactly one intent in every scenario; a resumed intent has
    the same digest.
  - Its EXEC_READY record is written once, or twice only when the first
    write was lost.
  - The replay matches it.
- **Native rejection** (AC-CAP-07-06). The bad-block evidence is `ff6fa301`
  and the rejected-intent digest is `f8c3247d`. No marker, NATIVE_DECIDED
  record or obligation follows, and the head stays at the genesis.
- **Re-import.** After every scenario, a restart and a re-import of the
  chain open no observation and no decision.

## Limitations

The README "Limits" section explains each of them:
- The fixture is before Plato and has one validator, so the fail-closed
  finality reads are proven by unit tests with a stand-in for Parlia.
- No `SIDE` decision occurs on the sequential profile.
- `ErrTransition`, a stored bad block and `ErrReplay` are covered by unit
  tests or by source review.
- The `skipBlock` patch and the refusal of a hook without a decision step
  are not exercised.
- No store or native write error is injected.
- A native bad-block record is keyed only by the block hash. A record left by an earlier attempt would also turn a durable intent of that block into a rejection.
- Native chain data has process-crash semantics (D100).
- The mirror is a probe stand-in (E05-01.registry). The node wiring belongs
  to E01-02.lifecycle_seams.
- Retained `SIDE` blocks wait for E05-01.activation_gc (block 0).
